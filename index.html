About the Association

The Home of Roller Sports in Chhattisgarh

Since 2015, and affiliated to RSFI since 2017, CGRSA has been the state's governing body for roller skating — conducting district and state championships, selecting teams for national events, and building a strong culture of discipline, fitness and sportsmanship.

✓State & district championships conducted under RSFI rules
✓Selection and training of Team Chhattisgarh for Nationals
✓Coach development and certified technical officials
✓Affiliated clubs and academies across the state
