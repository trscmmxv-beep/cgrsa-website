About the Association

The Home of Roller Sports in Chhattisgarh

Since 2015, and affiliated to RSFI since 2017, CGRSA has been the state's governing body for roller skating — conducting district and state championships, selecting teams for national events, and building a strong culture of discipline, fitness and sportsmanship.

✓State & district championships conducted under RSFI rules
✓Selection and training of Team Chhattisgarh for Nationals
✓Coach development and certified technical officials
✓Affiliated clubs and academies across the state

Getting Started

How to Start Roller Skating in Chhattisgarh

The path from a skater’s first lessons to state and national championships.

Step 1

Find a club or coach

Train with a CGRSA-affiliated club or coach in your district. Browse the clubs and coaches directory.

 Step 2

Register with RSFI

Every skater needs a current RSFI annual registration to take part in club, district, state and national championships.

 Step 3

Compete in your district

District associations conduct district championships and select skaters for state events.

 Step 4

Represent Chhattisgarh

Skaters selected at the Chhattisgarh State Roller Skating Championship represent the state at the RSFI National Championships.

Questions about starting out? Call +91 94255 52054 or email cgsrsa@gmail.com.
