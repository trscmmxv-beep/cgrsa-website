Affiliation

Join CGRSA

Clubs, coaches and district associations can apply for affiliation with the Chhattisgarh Roller Skating Association.

Register a club

Affiliated clubs can enter skaters in CGRSA district and state championships and are listed in the club directory.

Who can apply

  • Skating clubs and academies based in Chhattisgarh
  • With a regular training venue and a named coach
  • Affiliated through their district association where one exists

Documents to attach

  • Club registration certificate or society/trust papers, if any
  • Club logo (PNG or JPG)
  • List of office bearers and coaches
  • Photos of coaches (for the directory)
  • Recommendation letter from the district association

Process

  1. Submit the application below.
  2. The CGRSA office checks your details and documents.
  3. Pay the affiliation fee as advised by the office.
  4. Receive your affiliation letter and get listed on cgskate.com.

Affiliation fee: as notified by CGRSA. Call +91 94255 52054 for details.

Register as a coach

Registered coaches are listed with their club on cgskate.com and can accompany skaters at CGRSA championships.

Who can apply

  • Coaches working with an affiliated club or academy in Chhattisgarh
  • With coaching experience or certification in roller sports

Documents to attach

  • Passport-size photo (for the website)
  • Coaching certificates, if any
  • Letter from your club confirming your role
  • RSFI registration, if any

Process

  1. Submit the application below.
  2. The CGRSA office checks your details and documents.
  3. Pay the affiliation fee as advised by the office.
  4. Receive your affiliation letter and get listed on cgskate.com.

Affiliate a district body

Each district has one CGRSA-affiliated association that runs district championships and selects skaters for state events.

Who can apply

  • A registered roller sports association for one district of Chhattisgarh
  • With elected office bearers and at least one club in the district

Documents to attach

  • Society registration certificate
  • Constitution / bye-laws
  • List of office bearers with photos
  • Association logo
  • List of clubs in the district
  • Minutes of the meeting that resolved to seek affiliation

Process

  1. Submit the application below.
  2. The CGRSA office checks your details and documents.
  3. Pay the affiliation fee as advised by the office.
  4. Receive your affiliation letter and get listed on cgskate.com.
